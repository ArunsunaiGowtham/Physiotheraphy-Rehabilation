Pioneering Physical Rehabilitation Since 2008

Discover our clinical heritage, patient-first philosophy, and clinical leadership.

Our Founding Purpose

Dedicated to Restoring Movement, Dignity, and Vitality

PhysioLife was founded by Dr. Sarah Jenkins and Dr. Marcus Vance with a clear mandate: eliminate the rushed, impersonal conveyor-belt model of therapy and replace it with dedicated, one-on-one doctor care rooted in biomechanical diagnostics.

Our Mission

Deliver personalized, evidence-based orthopedic and neurological rehabilitation that permanently resolves root biomechanical dysfunctions.

Our Vision

To be the global benchmark for patient-centered physical therapy where state-of-the-art technology empowers lifelong mobility.

Our Journey

Clinic Milestones & Evolution

From a modest two-room physical therapy practice to an internationally recognized clinical institute.

2008 – The Beginning

PhysioLife Clinic Opens in New York

Founded by Dr. Sarah Jenkins with 2 treatment tables and a strict commitment to 60-minute 1-on-1 doctor appointments.

2014 – Sports Expansion

Launch of Sports Performance & ACL Lab

Added high-speed camera gait analysis and partnered with collegiate athletic teams for comprehensive injury prevention screening.

2019 – Advanced Technology Integration

Robotic Spinal Decompression & Laser Therapy

Invested in computerized spinal traction and class IV deep-tissue laser therapy to treat complex non-surgical disc herniations.

2023 – 15,000 Recoveries

Surpassed 15,000 Successfully Discharged Patients

Achieved a verified 98.4% patient satisfaction and recovery score independently audited by Healthcare Quality Partners.

2026 – Digital Care Portal Launch

Next-Gen Patient Portal & Tele-Rehab Hub

Launched our proprietary patient dashboard providing HD exercise video prescriptions and instant therapist messaging.

Guiding Principles

Our Core Clinical Values

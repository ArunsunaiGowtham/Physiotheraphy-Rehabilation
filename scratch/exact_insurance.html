How Our Insurance Verification Works

Zero paperwork stress. Our billing coordinators handle authorization and claim submissions directly on your behalf.

01
Submit Card Details

Fill out our quick secure online verification form below or bring your insurance card to clinic.

02
Benefit Eligibility Check

Our coordinators contact your insurer to verify in-network coverage, deductibles, and visit limits.

03
Transparent Copay Notice

Before your first visit, we provide you with a written explanation of your exact out-of-pocket copay.

04
Direct Claims Filing

We submit all clinical documentation and billing codes directly. You only pay your confirmed copay.

What to Bring to Your First Visit

To expedite your check-in and ensure immediate insurance authorization, please have the following items ready:

Government-Issued Photo ID

Driver's License, State ID, or Passport matching your insurance records.

Current Health Insurance Card

Both physical or digital copy of primary and secondary coverage cards.

Doctor's Referral / Prescription (If Required)

Many plans allow Direct Access without referral; we will confirm if your plan requires one.

Diagnostic Imaging Reports

Any recent MRI, X-ray, or surgical operation notes related to your condition.

Orthopedic Specialization

Spine, Neck & Sciatica Physical Rehabilitation

Non-surgical decompression, targeted manual mobilization, and postural realignment.

Clinical Overview & Diagnostic Approach

Spine and neck conditions represent over 40% of all musculoskeletal dysfunctions. At PhysioLife, our board-certified physical therapists utilize a specialized orthopedic diagnostic framework to distinguish whether your pain stems from disc herniation, facet joint arthrosis, neural tension, or postural muscle fatigue.

Rather than merely addressing localized spasms with passive heat packs, we apply progressive McKenzie spinal extension techniques, computerized traction, and stabilizing multifidus muscle retraining to decompress pinched nerves and permanently restore painless spinal movement.

Post-Spinal Fusion & Discectomy Recovery

Anatomical Focus & Modalities

Hands-On Manual Therapy

Grade III & IV passive vertebral mobilizations to open neural foramina and reduce facet restriction.

Computerized Spinal Decompression

Gentle cyclical axial distraction creating negative intradiscal pressure to facilitate disc retraction.

Myofascial Trigger Point Dry Needling

Deactivates deep muscular knots in the piriformis, quadratus lumborum, and trapezius muscles.

Terms of Clinical Care & Patient Agreement

Clear policies, informed consent, and mutual expectations designed to ensure safe, effective, and collaborative physical rehabilitation.

Clinical Agreement • Last Revised: January 1, 2026

1. Patient Care Relationship & Scope

Welcome to PhysioLife Rehabilitation Clinic. By scheduling appointments, receiving clinical treatments, or accessing our rehabilitation facilities, you agree to these Terms of Care.

All therapeutic interventions are directed by licensed Doctors of Physical Therapy (DPT) and licensed Physical Therapist Assistants (PTA) adhering to state medical licensing boards and professional ethical guidelines.

2. Informed Consent for Physical Therapy

Physical therapy involves active rehabilitation, functional exercise, manual joint mobilization, soft tissue therapy, and modalities (such as therapeutic ultrasound, heat/ice, or electrical stimulation).

  • Therapy Response: You acknowledge that while therapy is structured to restore pain-free movement, mild muscle soreness or temporary stiffness is a normal part of tissue healing.
  • Patient Autonomy: You maintain the right to ask questions, request modifications, or pause/decline any exercise or technique at any point during your session.

3. Scheduling, Cancellations & Late Arrivals

To provide each patient dedicated 1-on-1 attention and preserve open slots for patients requiring acute post-surgical rehab:

24-Hour Cancellation Notice

We request at least 24 hours advance notice for cancellations or rescheduling so another patient in pain may receive care.

Missed Session Fee

Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice may incur a standard $25 late cancellation fee. Genuine medical emergencies are always excused.

4. Patient Responsibilities

Optimal recovery requires active collaboration. Patients are expected to:

  • Disclose Health Conditions: Provide accurate medical history, active medications, surgical dates, cardiovascular risks, and any recent physical changes.
  • Follow Home Protocols: Adhere to home exercises (HEP) assigned by your therapist for continuous functional progress between clinic visits.
  • Appropriate Attire: Wear comfortable athletic clothing and supportive sneakers suitable for safe mobility, gait analysis, and strength exercises.

5. Financial Policy & Insurance Billing

We bill accepted in-network insurance providers directly on your behalf. However:

Copayments & Deductibles: Required copays, coinsurance, or deductible balances are collected at the time of each clinical session.
Non-Covered Services: If your insurance plan denies coverage or exceeds annual physical therapy benefit limits, the remaining balance becomes the patient's responsibility.

6. Non-Emergency Medical Notice

PhysioLife is an outpatient rehabilitation clinic and does not provide immediate trauma, acute cardiac, or emergency medical life-support services. If you experience chest pain, sudden numbness, severe shortness of breath, or an acute health crisis, call 911 immediately or visit the nearest hospital emergency room.

If you have any questions regarding your treatment plan, care policies, or billing arrangements, our patient coordinators are here to assist you:

HIPAA & Patient Privacy Policy

PhysioLife Rehabilitation Clinic is committed to safeguarding your Protected Health Information (PHI) and honoring your clinical privacy rights.

HIPAA Compliant • Last Revised: January 1, 2026

1. Our Clinical Privacy Commitment

At PhysioLife Rehabilitation Clinic, we hold your medical confidentiality to the highest standard. This Privacy Policy outlines how your Protected Health Information (PHI) and personal records are collected, utilized, and safeguarded in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and applicable state health laws.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or clinical information to any third-party advertisers or brokers under any circumstances.

2. Information We Collect

To deliver individualized physical therapy and coordinate with your care team, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Clinical & Health History: Injury background, physician referrals, diagnostic imaging reports, subjective pain ratings, range-of-motion assessments, and daily progress notes.
  • Contact & Identification: Legal full name, date of birth, mailing address, primary phone, email address, and emergency contact details.
  • Insurance & Billing Records: Policyholder details, insurance member IDs, group numbers, copay records, and claims billing history.

3. How We Use & Disclose Information

Your clinical data is strictly utilized for legitimate healthcare and rehabilitation purposes:

Treatment & Care

Collaborating with your referring orthopedic surgeons, primary doctors, and our physical therapists to design personalized recovery plans.

Direct Billing

Submitting claims, pre-authorizations, and proof of care to verified health insurance carriers to secure coverage.

Reminders & Telehealth

Sending SMS or email appointment confirmations, home exercise guidance (HEP), and patient portal updates.

Legal & Regulatory

Complying with statutory reporting mandates, public health oversight, or lawful court subpoenas when required by law.

4. Your Patient Rights Under HIPAA

As a patient of PhysioLife Rehabilitation Clinic, you have fundamental statutory rights regarding your health data:

Right to Inspect & Copy

You may inspect or request a paper or digital copy of your clinical chart, billing invoices, and therapy logs.

Right to Request Corrections

You have the right to request amendments to your health records if you believe information is inaccurate or incomplete.

Right to Confidential Communication

You may specify preferred communication channels (e.g., patient portal message only, specific mobile number).

Right to Accounting of Disclosures

You can obtain a list of non-standard instances where your health information was disclosed outside of routine care or billing.

5. Digital Security & EMR Safeguards

PhysioLife implements industry-leading technological, administrative, and physical security measures:

  • AES 256-Bit Encryption: Applied to all patient data at rest and in transit across our portal.
  • Role-Based Access: Only clinical staff directly assigned to your care have permission to review your chart.
  • Audit Logs & Monitoring: Every access to digital medical records is logged and periodically audited for compliance.
